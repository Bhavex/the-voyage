# World Bible (summary)

The full, living version is the Claude Doc "The Voyage — Informal Portfolio World Bible":
https://claude.ai/artifact/YJuzHkfskibppoqLw93aj1

## Locked decisions
- Painterly stylised, anime-film look (not low-poly, not photoreal)
- Free sailing with the wheel, plus sea-chart quick travel
- At each island: short cinematic fly-in, then dock and walk a small hand-crafted area
- Visitor plays a stylised captain version of Bhavishya, third-person
- Desktop first; phones get a lighter mode; a Quick Voyage overview for people in a hurry

## Islands
1. Film Island (prototype): open-air cinema, lighthouse, reel-into-projector interaction
2. Forge Island: 3D/Blender work on anvil pedestals
3. Editing Island: monitor wall of edits, before/after lever
4. Builder's Port: each web/dev project is a docked ship to board
5. Market Isle: finance and quant work, ticker board, ledger
6. The Uncharted Zone: work in progress in the fog, seen through the spyglass
7. Home Island: captain's cabin, About, message-in-a-bottle contact
8. Sky Island (hidden): unlocked by finding a secret on every island

## Four layers
Open sea → approach (fly-in) → on the island (walk) → the project (touch a landmark)
